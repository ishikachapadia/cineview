About Skull TV

Fear is an art form, we just give it a platform.

Skull TV was built for the ones who stay up late the ones who love stories that unsettle, provoke, and make your pulse race. We believe horror isn't cheap thrills it's cinema at its most honest. What scares us reveals who we are.

From psychological masterpieces to indie gems, we curate films that go beyond blood and shadows. Our mission is to create a space where filmmakers, actors, and fans connect through their love for the strange and the beautiful because horror deserves respect.

Whether you're hunting for your next obsession or rediscovering a forgotten classic, Skull TV is your gateway to everything hauntingly good. Welcome to the darker side of cinema.
